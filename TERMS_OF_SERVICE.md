## 1. What CloudDrive Mount is

CloudDrive Mount mounts remote storage — SFTP, FTP/FTPS, WebDAV, Google Drive, and Cloudflare R2 — as local drives on your Windows computer. It is local software: it has no OceanSideVR account, no OceanSideVR servers, and no online service component. The app connects only to the storage services you configure.
